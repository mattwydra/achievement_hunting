Defeat Bed of Chaos

Destroy the Bed of Chaos, source of the Witch of Izalith’s tragic legacy.

Medium

Overview

The Bed of Chaos is the final remnant of the Witch of Izalith’s failed attempt to recreate the First Flame. You must navigate a treacherous arena to reach and destroy its two glowing cores, then finish off the exposed heart at the center.

This boss is notorious for its hazardous environment—collapsing floors, rotating tree limbs, and an instant-death grab attack make progression more punishing than the boss’s own attacks.

How to Access the Fight

  • Place the Lordvessel and travel to Lost Izalith via the shortcut unlocked by the Chaos Servant covenant (optional but speeds access).
  • Make your way through the Demon Ruins to the entrance of Lost Izalith.
  • Proceed through the area until you reach the boss arena—a large, circular chamber with a crumbling floor.
  • You can also warp directly to the Lost Izalith shortcut bonfire if already unlocked.

Boss Strategy: Bed of Chaos

  1. Seed Nodes: Dodge the sweeping branches to reach each glowing seed embedded in the floor. There is one on each side, it does not matter which you destroy first. After breaking one seeding, the ground to collapses, creating new paths.
  2. Annoyance ensues: As you try to reach the other side, you will likely have to jump over gaps. Sometimes, you can be lucky enought to just run it, but this is unlikely to work.
  3. Fight the Heart: After both seeds are destroyed, the central heart is exposed. You will approach the center, and the ground will give out. You will need to jump onto the branch that is exposed as a result of the collapsed floor. Upon landing on the branch, be wary of the arms continuing swings as you climb up. After climbing up, you will need to break several branches to approach the core. Be wary of the pillars of lava that will be spawned, ensure you have plenty estus and try to avoid them if you can.
  4. Defeat Bed of Chaos: Upon entering the core, one swing should be enough to deal the finishing blow, and put the Witch of Izalith out of her misery.
  5. See full boss guide: Bed of Chaos Boss Guide.

Lore: The Tragedy of Izalith

The Witch of Izalith and her daughters—Quelaag and the Fair Lady—sought to replicate the First Flame to stave off the coming Age of Dark. Their experiment backfired catastrophically, birthing the chaotic nightmare that became the Bed of Chaos.

As the flame warped flesh and wood alike, the sisters were transformed: Quelaag became a half-spider, and the Fair Lady, a mute being trapped within the chaos. The land itself was twisted, spawning demons and deformities that roam Lost Izalith.

Destroying the Bed of Chaos is both an act of mercy and necessity—to end its endless cycle of corruption and honor the sacrifices of those who fell to its madness.
